Our Story & Background

How Nirmaan Foundation Was Born

Founded in 2014, Nirmaan Foundation started as a small community initiative driven by university students. Today, we have empowered over 30,000 individuals across 18+ cities.

{% if has_story %}
{{ our_story.content|linebreaks }}
{% else %}

Founded in 2014 in Bengaluru, Nirmaan Foundation was created when a group of 15 college volunteers witnessed the severe educational disparities in nearby municipal schools. Moved by the students' enthusiasm and hunger for learning, they began conducting weekend literacy classes and mentoring sessions.

Over the past decade, that small weekend circle evolved into a nationwide movement. By mobilizing youth volunteers, partnering with CSR stakeholders, and building low-cost digital classrooms, Nirmaan Foundation now provides holistic education, nutrition, and mentorship to children from underrepresented backgrounds across India.

2014

The Foundation Begins

Started in Bengaluru with 15 passionate college students teaching weekend foundational math and reading in two underserved municipal schools.

2017

Strong Foundations Program Launch

Formalized structured weekend literacy curricula, scaling to 12 government schools and engaging over 500 volunteer mentors.

2020

Digital Learning Hubs

Pioneered low-cost digital classrooms and tablet learning centers to bridge the digital divide during school lockdowns.

2024–Present

Nationwide Expansion

Operating across 18+ cities with 7,500+ active volunteers, touching 30,000+ young lives every single weekend.
