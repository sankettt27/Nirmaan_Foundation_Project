2014

The Foundation Begins

Started in Bengaluru with 15 passionate college students teaching weekend foundational math and reading in two underserved municipal schools.

2017

Strong Foundations Program Launch

Formalized structured weekend literacy curricula, scaling to 12 government schools and engaging over 500 volunteer mentors.

2020

Digital Learning Hubs

Pioneered low-cost digital classrooms and tablet learning centers to bridge the digital divide during school lockdowns.

2024–Present

Nationwide Expansion

Operating across 18+ cities with 7,500+ active volunteers, touching 30,000+ young lives every single weekend.
