The Problem

Thousands of Children Risk Being Left Behind

Despite progress, millions of children in India still lack access to quality education, safe learning environments, and opportunities to realise their full potential. Nirmaan Foundation exists to change this.

01
Opportunity Gaps in Learning

Over 40% of children in low-income communities leave school before Class 8 due to lack of support and engagement. Early dropout robs children of a lifetime of opportunity.

02
Underfunded Classrooms

Government schools in urban slums and rural areas are chronically understaffed and under-resourced. One teacher for 80 students cannot deliver quality education.

03
Missing Holistic Development

Academic learning alone isn't enough. Children in underserved communities need sports, arts, life-skills, and emotional support — areas severely neglected today.
