Primary Education

Ignite — Foundational Literacy & Numeracy

Weekend and after-school supplementary teaching delivered by trained youth volunteers in government schools to ensure all children attain grade-level competence in reading, basic math, and conversational English by Grade 5.

Curriculum & Methodology

Activity-based learning (ABL), phonics modules, fun bilingual storybooks, and diagnostic quarterly skill assessments aligned with NIPUN Bharat standards.

Reach: 18,500+ Children across 90+ Schools

Mentorship & Careers

Disha — Youth Mentorship & Higher Education Scholarships

1-on-1 career guidance, soft-skills workshops, entrance exam preparation, and need-based financial scholarships for adolescent girls and youth transitioning from secondary school to university.

Curriculum & Methodology

Corporate mentor pairings, financial literacy clinics, communication training, and 100% college enrolment guarantee tracking.

The Nirmaan Approach

How Our Educational Model Delivers Long-Term Results

We collaborate deeply with state education boards, local community elders, and dedicated corporate partners to ensure sustainable, systemic change.

Continuous Diagnostic Testing

We test every student at baseline, midline, and endline using standardized ASER-calibrated assessments to tailor tutoring.

Certified Volunteer Training

Volunteers undergo a 14-day rigorous pedagogical induction covering classroom dynamics, child protection, and empathetic pedagogy.

Government School Integration

Rather than running parallel systems, we work directly inside government schools, enhancing existing classrooms and teacher capacity.
